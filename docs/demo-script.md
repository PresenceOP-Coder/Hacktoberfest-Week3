A high-impact, step-by-step 60-second video and presentation script showing the core value of SnoozeBox: offline local AI, radical rest, thread deduplication, and zero-anxiety triage.

### Step 1: 00:00 – 00:12 | The Morning Briefing & Hero Wake-Up
1. **Screen:** Open **Morning** (`http://localhost:5173`).
2. **Action:**
   - Point out the large sleeping Snorlax in the hero panel blending into the teal background with drifting `Z` glyphs.
   - Read Snorlax's sleepy speech bubble: *"Morning. Only one thing is worth waking up for today. The rest I handled while you slept."*
   - Highlight the single natural summary sentence: *"Since you started, I've saved you about 3 minutes of scrolling and quietly put 2 newsletters to bed."*
   - Click the **"Wake me up"** button directly below the bubble to play the gentle Poké Flute melody and refresh the digest.

### Step 3: 00:24 – 00:38 | Attention Debt & Thread Evolution

2. **Action:**
